## Hardware-in-the-loop (real firmware ELF on simavr ATmega328P)

`sim/hil/`: the compiled `canary.elf` runs on simavr (upstream master
5b08da6, which carries the TWI master address-ACK fix that 1.6 lacks) with
virtual SGP40/SHT40/SCD41 on the TWI bus (datasheet commands, CRC-8,
measurement-time NACKs), NTC voltages on ADC0/ADC1 and the button on PD2. What
is checked is what a person would see and hear: LED PWM duty, the sounder
drive (PB1 edges counted), and the telemetry.

| Scenario | What happens | Checks | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| normal_charge | pack warms 22 → 34 °C over 25 min; self-test press | never WARN/ALARM/FAULT; green breathes; self-test passes and beeps | **7/7 pass** |
| cleaning_spray | VOC spike, nothing else | WARN, never ALARM, back to NORMAL | **5/5 pass** |
| breath | CO₂ +2500 ppm for ~20 s, trace VOC | never ALARM | **3/3 pass** |
| vent | solvent + CO₂ at 400 s, then self-heating | ALARM **7.6 s** after onset; siren 4 kHz (measured 3997 Hz); T3 duty 0.38; red flashing | **8/8 pass** |
| overheat | pack +4 °C/min, no gas | ALARM 52.6 s after onset (≤ 120 s) | **8/8 pass** |
| hush | vent, HUSH at +40 s, heat joins at +120 s | siren off while hushed, red keeps flashing, siren returns when it gets worse | **11/11 pass** |
| sensor_fault | SGP40 stops answering | FAULT within 1.1 s, amber double blink, chirp | **5/5 pass** |
| probe | remote probe heats 4.5 °C/min, tab stays cool | ALARM 81.6 s after onset; probe reading used | **9/9 pass** |
| **Total** | | | **56/56 pass** |

Plots: `sim/hil/out/<scenario>.png` (regenerated by `scenarios.py`).
