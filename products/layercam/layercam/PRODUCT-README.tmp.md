# LayerCam

A camera for the inside of a Prusa CORE One: it fits Prusa's own printed
Buddy3D camera case, feeds Prusa Connect, sees the bed in daylight and in
infrared, and makes a per-layer timelapse that it uploads to a NAS or S3.
