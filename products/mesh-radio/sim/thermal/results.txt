--- Charger NTC (TS) window: BQ25185 TS/MR pin, NTC10K = Murata NCP15XH103F03RC straight to GND (circuit.py)
  TS bias ITSMR 36.5/38/39.5 uA (SLUSF65B p.8); VHOT 0.105/0.115/0.125 V (falling), VCOLD 0.9575/1.0075/1.0575 V (rising); exits VHOT_EXIT 0.135, VCOLD_EXIT 0.820 V typ
  hot stop  typ 59.5 C (spread 55.6 .. 63.8 C, NTC 1 % and B tolerance not included)
  cold stop typ 1.4 C (spread -0.6 .. 3.4 C, NTC 1 % and B tolerance not included)

  => CHG_TS reaches the nRF52840 (U8, an analog input): firmware can read the NTC and apply the 0-45 C rule through /CE.
  => no single resistor (series or parallel) moves the hot stop to 45 C and keeps the cold stop at 0 C with a B3380 NTC: the window is a fixed ~0/60 C design (no JEITA in the BQ25185).

STUDY 3: cold start at -40 C

Outside their range at -40 C: JST PH/GH/SH (-25 C) [accepted, owner decision 3]; Amphenol USB-C if its true lower limit is -20 C (unverified).  Every IC, crystal, button and passive covers -40 C.

  ABS07 32.768 kHz: ESR <= 70 kOhm at -40..85 C (ABS07 p.1) vs nRF52840 RS_LFXO <= 100 kOhm (PS p.97): PASS.
  ABS07 frequency: -0.036 ppm/C^2 parabola -> -152 ppm at -40 C, -130 ppm at +85 C, -44 ppm at 60 C (plus +-20 ppm tolerance).  The Adafruit core declares the LFXO as NRF_CLOCK_LF_ACCURACY_20_PPM (bluefruit.cpp, USE_LFXO): Bluetooth timing then assumes 20 ppm while the crystal is off by up to ~170 ppm -> dropped BLE connections when cold or hot.  MARGINAL; fix in the board variant: declare 250 ppm (or 150 ppm).
  TCXO ECS-TXO-20CSMV4-320-AP: -40..+105 C, +-0.5 ppm, start-up 2 ms (p.1): PASS (firmware TCXO delay >= 5 ms).

Battery: a LiPo cell at -40 C cannot deliver 2-3 A bursts (internal resistance rises 5-10x below -20 C); the burst study's worn-cell case (0.3 Ohm) is the nearest proxy.  Firmware should cap power below -20 C.
Mechanical:
  U.FL (Hirose, -40..+90 C): fine thermally; it unmates under shock/vibration (drone drop): add a retention clip or a dab of staking compound over the mated plug.
  0908SQ air-core choke (L14): tinned copper coil on copper pads, CTE close to the board (17 vs 14-17 ppm/C), the spring coil is compliant, so thermal cycling is a small risk; shock is the bigger one (a 2-3 mm tall coil on two joints).  Stake it with a dot of low-loss RF adhesive; do not let adhesive fill the coil (it shifts the inductance).
  WLCSP parts (TPS63810, TPS62840, TPS22950, SSM10N954L) and the nRF52840 aQFN: per-burst swings at the TPS63810 are 12-70 C (study 1, by power), 500 k cycles a year at one message a minute; with drop shock, underfill (or corner bonding) of the WLCSPs is advisable for the field build.
