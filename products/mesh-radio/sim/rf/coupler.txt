Timing: 1 nF || 100 k decays with tau = 100 us; it charges through the diode (~1 k at these currents) in ~1 us.
The reading settles within ~0.3 ms of PA_EN; sample at 0.5-1 ms.  The nRF52840 SAADC (tacq 10-40 us) samples a
100 k source with < 1 LSB error at tacq 40 us (Nordic: 40 us allows up to 800 k).  100 k also keeps the detector
load ~50 k at RF, far above the ~50 ohm coupled line, so the coupled port stays terminated by the 49.9 R end.
