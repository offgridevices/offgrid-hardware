   a ~10 mm line edge-coupled (0.15 mm gap) to the U.FL feed after the LFCN; coupled-port end ->

   beta*l = 0.35 rad: C ~ 20log(k sin(beta l)) ~ -26 dB; directivity of a short microstrip coupler ~10-15 dB.
   30 dBm, good antenna (RL 20 dB)  coupled reverse wave  -7.4 dBm
   30 dBm, poor antenna (VSWR 2)    coupled reverse wave  -3.9 dBm
   30 dBm, antenna missing          coupled reverse wave  +4.3 dBm

      PA_EN in every burst; above threshold drop VPA to 2.7 V and SX1262 drive, flag "no antenna".
