  Release after OCD / SCD needs BAT - V- > 1 V (checked after the 8 ms recovery delay): the BQ29700 sinks
  8-24 uA from V- to VSS.  Once the cell is cut off, the board's supply (BAT_P to GND) collapses, its
  regulators fall into UVLO and its load drops; the protector releases only if the board then draws less
  than the 8-24 uA sink at ~1 V.  With the short still present it re-trips (hiccup, ~28 ms period); with
  it removed the board restarts - IF the collapsed board draws < 8 uA at 1 V.  The leakage of the parts on
  BAT_P (charger IQ_BAT 4-5 uA, TPS2121 standby up to 25 uA per input, the 1 M / 1.5 M divider, eFuse
  reverse leakage) is of that order, so restart on the cell alone is NOT guaranteed: bench check.  USB,
  solar or drone power always restarts it (a charger pulls V- below VSS).
