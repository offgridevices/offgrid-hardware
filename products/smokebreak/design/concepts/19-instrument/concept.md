# 19 — Instrument

**Pitch.** See the engineering: the power board is the hero, suspended in smoked glass on a machined aluminium base, like a hi-fi preamp. It is trusted because you can see it is not a cheap board.

**Form & size.** 96 × 64 × 26 mm, ~190 g. 8 mm CNC aluminium base (heatsink and weight, rubber feet). Machined, vapour-polished smoked polycarbonate shell, with a 2 mm smoked glass top bonded in. Batch 1: CNC; later moulded smoked PC and a die-cast base.

**Boards.** Two. The power board (88 × 56 mm, 2 oz, ENIG) is laid out to be looked at: clean blocks, gold vias, labelled sections. It sits on a thermal pad on the base. The logic board (42 × 36 mm) sits on 4 brass standoffs 10 mm above it and carries the 1.3" OLED just under the glass, the MCU, USB-C (back) and 3 side-firing RGB LEDs. A 10-pin 2 mm header joins the boards. XT60/XT30 male pass through the left wall; the drone leads exit the right wall through grommets.

**UX.** Three machined steel keys on the front edge, *Bind · Power · Limit*, each named on the glass above it. Power is the biggest, centred under the ring. The Beacon Ring is laser-etched into the top glass and edge-lit, so it floats. The dead-front OLED is invisible until it has something to say. The press logic is as in the spec. First use: battery in (the screen surfaces) → drone in → Power. Answers pains 7, 6 and 2 (trust).

**Cost delta.** Base +$6, shell +$5, steel keys +$1.5, second board and header +$1 → about +$14.

**Risks.** Scratches and fingerprints (hard-coat); OLED brightness through the tint in sunlight; the internals must be cosmetically perfect; front presses push the unit (weight and feet).
