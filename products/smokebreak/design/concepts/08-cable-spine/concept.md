# 08 Cable spine

**Pitch:** the leads and the electronics form one flat, soft sleeve. When it is off it is a plain black cable; when it wakes, the words and the Beacon Ring shine up through the silicone.

**Form & size:** a 100 x 30 x 9.5 mm spine in a ~300 mm sleeve, ~75 g with tails. Soft-touch LSR silicone overmoulded on a potted core, with a thin smoked skin over the display. Batch 1: cast silicone in a printed mould, or MJF TPU; later, LSR overmould.

**Boards:** one flat 26 x 90 mm 2-oz board. The FETs sit at the lead pads, so the 14 AWG copper spreads the switch heat. A 0.96" OLED lies flat behind the skin. USB-C sits under a lip on the front edge. Flat bonded twin leads, Y-split at each end: XT60 + XT30 male (battery side) and female (drone side).

**UX:** dead-front, so nothing shows until it is lit. Power is a soft dome inside the ring. Limit and Bind are two smaller domes with printed names; the Limit step shows on screen, and 20 A asks for a second press. Battery and Drone arrows are at each end. First use: battery onto the left tail, drone onto the right, press the ring dome. It drapes over a frame or lies flat, so it never tips or pulls on the drone. Answers pain points 6 and 8.

**Changes to the spec:** both sides on tails; the face is silicone, not PCB-under-PC.

**Cost delta:** about +$3.50 (LSR tooling about $6k, potting, second tail, a 4-way Y).

**Risks:** a thin pottable stack (TVS and USB-C are the tallest parts); repairability; seeing the OLED through the smoked skin; 20 A heat inside silicone.
