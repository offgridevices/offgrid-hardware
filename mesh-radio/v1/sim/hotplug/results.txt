  USB sources may legally sit at up to 5.25 V (USB 2.0) / 5.5 V (Type-C vSafe5V); the USB OV cut-off is
  5.22-5.57 V (comparator and 1 %% resistor tolerance): a 5.25-5.5 V charger can be refused.  Once tripped,
  OVLO only releases below 1.09 V on the pin, i.e. 4.89 V on VBUS.
