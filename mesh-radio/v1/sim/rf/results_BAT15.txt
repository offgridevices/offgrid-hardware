====================================================================================================
STUDY 8  Missing-antenna ratio detector: coupler (5.5 mm, 0.10 mm gap) terminated in 49.9 R at both ends;
         REFL_DET = BAT15 at the U.FL end, FWD_DET = the same at the filter end, each into 1 nF || 100 k;
         firmware reads REFL_DET / FWD_DET   [COMPARISON RUN: RF_DIODE=BAT15]
====================================================================================================
Inputs.  Board: the antenna section of study 1 (coupler from coupler.py's field solve) inside the real chain: PA,
  LPF, switch, LFCG-1000+; both PA models, so the PA's own reflection of the returning wave is included.  Antenna at
  the U.FL: matched; VSWR 2 at every phase; missing (open, every phase = any cable length); 902/915/928 MHz.
  Power = the matched-antenna power at the U.FL: 27 dBm (the firmware's lowest checked level), 30 (US; the 1 W
  probe), 33, 35 and 36 dBm (3.6-4.0 V).  Temperatures -40, 0, 25, 60, 85 C.
  Diode BAT15: IS 1.15e-07 A (typ), N 1.080, RS 6.0 ohm, EG 0.55 eV, XTI 2; corners IS 9.80e-09 / 3.14e-06 A (VF limits at 1 mA),
  EG 0.50 / 0.69 eV; CJ 0.20 pF (0.24 max as a corner).  The two diodes are separate parts: unmatched pairings are run
  (7: reflected / forward = typ/typ, VF max/VF min, VF min/VF max, VF max/VF max, VF min/VF min, EG low/EG high, EG high/EG low).  BAT68: IF-VF curves at -40/25/85 C fitted (N 0.932, RS 9.2, EG 0.65, within 4 mV);
  the table's VF 318 typ / 340 max mV at 1 mA set IS; 296 mV ASSUMED as the low corner.  DC: mean diode current over
  an RF cycle = V / 100 k + leak (checked against ngspice below).  ADC floor: a reading below 20 mV is not trusted.
Criteria: a ratio threshold (fixed, or of the form R25 x exp(b (T-25) + c (P-30)) with the board temperature and the
  firmware's modelled power) must separate every missing case from every VSWR-2 case at every level and temperature,
  both PA models, every diode pairing: PASS with >= 1 dB of margin each side (and FWD_DET above the ADC floor),
  MARGINAL if the window is open but narrower, FAIL if it closes.
