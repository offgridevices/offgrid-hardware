A. Amplifier operating points (PA output = antenna + 1.1 dB, sim/rf)
====================================================================================================
Nominal: QPA9510 data sheet (23 Jun 2025) p.4 "PAE vs. Average Output Power", 915 MHz, VCC 3.6 V, VAPC 2.8 V,
  ICQ 215 mA, 25 C, 50 ohm, GSM bursts (p.3: Pin +4.5 dBm, 37.5 % duty).  Read: PAE 31.5 % at 30 dBm, 45 % at
  33 dBm, 56 % at 35 dBm, peak 57.5 % at 35.5 dBm.  Check against p.3: 35.4 dBm at 1.7 A from 3.6 V = 56.7 %.
  Current at 3.6 V from the curve (ICC = Pout / (PAE x VCC)): 30 dBm 0.88 A, 33 dBm 1.23 A, 35 dBm 1.57 A.
  Firmware lowers VPA at low power (plan: 2.7 V for 1 W).  ASSUMED: a saturated PA keeps its PAE when VCC and
  the output move together (output ~ VCC^2), so the curve is read at Pout - 20 log(VCC / 3.6 V), with VPA
  chosen for 35 dBm-equivalent (PAE 56 %), clamped to 2.7-4.0 V.  PAE at the case temperature (hot) is lower
  than this 25 C curve; not modelled.
