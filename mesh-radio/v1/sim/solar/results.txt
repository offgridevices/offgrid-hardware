  As drawn the LM66100 (CE = GND) is a closed switch between BAT_P and VSYS: SYS (regulated to 4.5 V, or
  pulled down by the cell) charges the cell directly, outside the charger's ISET, CV, /CE and TS control;
  only the BQ29700 (OVP 4.275 V after 1.25 s, OCC 4 A) still limits it.  With CE = VSYS the LM66100 only
  turns off once VSYS is 35 mV (0-80 mV) above the cell, i.e. above ~0.44 A reverse; a weak source (the
  panel) never gets there, so the cell still charges through it.  A Schottky in its place always blocks.
