(b) Back-EMF network
  One phase moving, others at 0: CMP gain 0.1620 (1/6.174); NEUTRAL gain 0.0601.
  All three together: CMP gain 0.1803 (1/5.545) - the unloaded 2.2/(10 + 2.2) divider.
  CMP_x - NEUTRAL = (V_x - mean of the three phases) / 6.545 (one phase moving: 0.1019 = (2/3)/6.545).  Equivalently, with NEUTRAL held, each phase sees (10k || 2.2k) / (10k + 10k || 2.2k) = 1/6.545.
  mid throttle (50 %), 60000 eRPM, 48 kHz PWM: CMP max 2.94 V, NEUTRAL -0.05..1.82 V, CMP min -0.13 V; zero-crossing error (PWM-on samples) <= 0.00 el. deg (ideal resistors)
  full throttle (98 %), 60000 eRPM, 48 kHz PWM: CMP max 2.97 V, NEUTRAL -0.05..2.05 V, CMP min -0.13 V; zero-crossing error (PWM-on samples) <= 0.00 el. deg (ideal resistors)
