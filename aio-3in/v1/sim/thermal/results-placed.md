## Conclusions (3 Oct, placed board, before routing)

**Dominant I^2 R paths.** At full60, 50 C, the copper makes 13.0 W against 9.5 W in the FETs.

- **VBAT and GND, 57 % between them.** Most of it is in the battery corner:
  - 60 A goes through the 12-13 vias at each battery pad: 1.0 W in the P_BAT+ barrels alone.
  - The planes then carry it out of that corner on In4 (VBAT) and In1 (GND).
  - The shunt-to-BAT- path is 1.7-2.0 mOhm for M2-M4, because their GND return has to come back to the corner.
- **The four M_SRC paths, 25 %.** The low-side sources to the shunt run through the B.Cu strip and the In3 pour:
  - M1_SRC is 3.2 mOhm (1.24 W), the others 1.9-2.2 mOhm;
  - these are the largest per-channel resistances on the board;
  - only heavier copper touches them (c: 1.3-2.2 mOhm).
- **Phases, 18 %.** M3A's high-side source to motor pad path is 2.0 mOhm against about 1 mOhm for the others.
- **Hot spots** are all within 4 mm of the battery pads (VBAT In4 / F.Cu, M1_SRC In3, GND F.Cu at R_S1).
  Q1AL, next to them, is always the hottest FET.

**Fixes at 50 C, prop wash, full60** (target: FET < 150 C, MCU < 105 C):

| Variant | FET C | MCU C | Meets target? |
|---|---|---|---|
| a: GND fill + stitching | 172 | 160 | No |
| b: a + battery-pad and R_S1 vias | 164 | 154 | No |
| c: b + 2 oz inner | 146 | 138 | FET only; MCU 33 K over |
| d: b + 2 oz outer | 156 | 148 | No |

None of a-d meets both. c is the only one that gets the FETs under 150 C.

- **The MCUs are the binding limit, not the FETs.** Each ESC MCU sits 6-12 K under its own channel's FETs, and
  the whole board is at 135-150 C.
- **With h halved,** every variant is 50-60 K hotter still.
- **e: AM32 at 100 C as a sustained cap (variant b copper).**
  - Prop wash: 32 % of full current per motor (4.8 A, about 47 % thrust).
  - h halved: 14 % (2.1 A, about 27 % thrust). That is below the 3.5 A hover, so it cannot sustain flight.
- **Per-channel derating** settles within ±0.04 of those common values but trades between channels. The quad
  can only fly the common value.

**Caveats.**
- The fill areas are an upper bound: there are no signal traces yet.
- Stitching is limited to the 105 mm² where F.Cu, In1 and B.Cu are all GND (107 vias).
- Via barrels now pass other nets' layers through antipads. This changed the base numbers by under 1 K.
- Thrust fraction is taken as (I/I_full)^(2/3), for a propeller.
