## What the driver's overcurrent monitor trips at

Copper in what it measures (20 C): low side 0.68 mOhm (channel 1 phase A, the most); high side up to 0.91 mOhm with only its own phase's current, and 0.99 mOhm when all four motors draw the same current through the battery plane, as in a punch.  A trip turns that motor's FETs off for 4 ms; AM32 is not told.  The hot FET is the hard 3-minute flight's hottest on a 50 C day (128 C).
