# 11 — Flip

**Pitch.** It is called SmokeBreak, so it opens like a lighter. Flip the brushed lid and it wakes; snap it shut and the drone is off instantly. The instructions live inside the lid.

**Form & size.** 84 × 52 × 16 mm body plus a 6.5 mm lid: 23 mm closed, ~115 g. Moulded soft-touch body (MJF for batch 1). The lid is CNC aluminium, brushed and graphite anodised, with the Beacon mark engraved. Stainless pin and spring-steel cam leaf: the Zippo clack.

**Boards.** One 80 × 48 mm 2 oz board under the face, carrying the 1.3" OLED, a 12 mm Power tact switch, a 6 mm Bind switch and a 24-detent encoder under a knurled steel wheel. A hall sensor sits under the lid magnet. XT60/XT30 male on the left, drone leads on the right, USB-C on the front edge (the hinge takes the back).

**UX.** Open the lid and the screen shows battery voltage. Power: press the button in the Beacon Ring (check, then on); press again or close the lid for off. Limit: roll the flint wheel; 20 A asks "Props off?", press Power to confirm. Bind: its own key. Steps 1-2-3 and "Close the lid: off" are printed inside the lid, always in view. Slamming the lid is the fastest panic-off there is. Closed, it survives a toolbox. Answers pain points 6 and 7.

**Cost delta.** CNC lid +$4, hinge/cam +$0.4, wheel and encoder +$0.8, hall +$0.3: ≈ +$5.5.

**Risks.** Hinge and cam fatigue. Motor magnets on the bench could fool the lid hall sensor (use a threshold plus a second sensor). Leads must clear the lid when open. The lid's edges must never touch live pins.

**Scores.** Premium 5 · Noob-proof 4 · Bench practicality 4 · Build quality 4 · Uniqueness 5
