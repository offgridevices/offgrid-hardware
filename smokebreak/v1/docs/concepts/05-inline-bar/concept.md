# 05 Inline bar

**Pitch:** a slim aluminium bar that lives in the power lead, like an inline fuse holder. Energy flows left to right, and so does everything you read and press.

**Form & size:** 128 x 24 x 15 mm bar, ~85 g with tails. Black anodised aluminium extrusion (it is also the heatsink for the switch), 0.7 mm toughened glass top, machined end caps, silicone strain-relief boots. Batch 1: cut extrusion with a CNC'd window and printed end caps; later, a production extrusion with moulded caps.

**Boards:** one long 20 x 120 mm 2-oz board. Power path at the drone end, pressed onto the extrusion floor with a thermal pad; logic and screen in the middle; USB-C mid-mount on the front flank.

**UX:** along the bar, left to right: Battery arrow, 0.96" colour IPS screen (160 x 80, words in colour), the Beacon Ring Power key, the Limit key with six lit pips (AUTO 1 2 5 10 20; 20 A asks for a second press), the Bind key, then the Drone arrow. First use: battery in, drone in, press the ring. It is noob-proof because the order of the face is the order of the task. Answers pain points 6 (words, not LED codes) and 8 (both connectors, flexible tails).

**Changes to the spec:** the battery side is on an 8 cm tail (XT60 + XT30 male), not panel-mounted, so the bar can lie in line.

**Cost delta:** about +$2.50 (extrusion, glass, IPS screen, second tail, pip LEDs).

**Risks:** strain on the tails (needs good boots); the unused live male pins swing on a tail, so they need shrouded plugs; 20 A for 30 s into a narrow extrusion is fine, but must be checked.

**Scores (1-5):** Premium 5 · Noob-proof 4 · Bench practicality 4 · Build quality 5 · Uniqueness 3
