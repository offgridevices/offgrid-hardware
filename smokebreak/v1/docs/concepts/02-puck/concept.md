# 02 Puck

**Pitch:** a heavy aluminium puck with a glass top. The Beacon Ring glows under the glass around a round screen. Press the glass to power on. The ring's dot is a real metal button, and it is Bind.

**Form & size:** Ø80 × 22 mm, about 150 g.
- CNC-machined, bead-blasted black anodised aluminium.
- Flats at 9 o'clock (battery) and 3 o'clock (drone).
- A Ø66 toughened glass top, printed on the back. Rubber foot ring.
- Batch 1: CNC, or MJF for samples. Later: die-cast and machined.

**Boards:**
- **One round 4-layer board, Ø74, 2 oz outer layers.** FETs, shunt and pulse resistors sit underneath on a gap pad over the base, which is the heatsink.
- The round IPS sits on its FPC under the glass, with a light guide over 12 RGB LEDs.
- XT60PW + XT30PW male are on the board edge at the left flat, with a rib between them. The leads leave through glands on the right. USB-C is on the back rim.

**UX:**
- **Power:** press anywhere on the glass (one dome switch, 0.4 mm travel).
- **Bind:** press the dot (stainless, glows blue).
- **Limit:** a pill on the front rim. Each click lights the next mark (AUTO 1 2 5 10 20), and 20 A needs a second click.
- **Screen:** a 1.28" round IPS with large words.
- **First use:** 1 Battery in, 2 Drone in, 3 Press the glass.
- **Pains answered (SPEC §2):** best on 6 and 7.

**Cost delta:** about +$7 (shell +$3.5, glass +$0.8, round IPS +$1.5, light guide and LEDs +$0.6, board +$0.6).

**Risks:**
- Tuning the feel of the glass click.
- Machining cost.
- The leads drag a light puck.

**Scores:** Premium 5 · Noob-proof 5 · Bench practicality 4 · Build quality 5 · Uniqueness 4
