# 07 Field clip

**Pitch:** a rugged tag you clip to your pack, so you can check a drone at the crash site before you plug a battery back in. Its tails stow by plugging into its own inputs.

**Form & size:** 96 x 54 x 19 mm (110 mm with the lug), ~95 g. Bead-blasted anodised aluminium shell, TPE lug with a stainless-gate carabiner, sealed silicone keys, a USB-C flap; aiming for IP54. Batch 1: MJF nylon shell with a cast TPE lug; later, aluminium with an overmoulded lug.

**Boards:** one 88 x 46 mm 2-oz board. Power path under the keys, logic under the screen. Battery males (XT60 + XT30, rib between) on the front flank, USB-C beside them under the flap, and the drone tails (12 cm) out of the right end through a boot.

**UX:** a 1.3" colour reflective (memory-in-pixel) LCD, readable in full sun. A big glove-friendly Beacon Ring Power key. Limit and Bind are large pill keys, with the Limit steps printed under Limit (20 A asks for a second press). Steps are printed along the bottom. First use: unclip and pull the tails from the inputs, plug in the battery and drone, press the ring. Stowed, the female pins are covered and nothing flaps. Answers pain points 2 and 7 out in the field.

**Cost delta:** about +$4.00 (memory LCD, carabiner, TPE, sealing).

**Risks:** sealing round the connectors; a self-plugged loop must never be powered (firmware ignores it, and there is no battery anyway); the memory LCD is slower than an OLED.

**Scores (1-5):** Premium 4 · Noob-proof 4 · Bench practicality 4 · Build quality 5 · Uniqueness 4
