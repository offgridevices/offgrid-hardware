# 06 Plug-through

**Pitch:** a rigid dongle with no leads at all. The battery plugs into one end and the drone's own pigtail into the other. The whole black-glass top is the Power key.

**Form & size:** 64 x 38 x 21 mm, ~70 g. Machined aluminium unibody, black glass top on a 4-point flexure over one central switch (a single trackpad-like click). Batch 1: CNC aluminium or MJF nylon with a glass insert; later, die-cast.

**Boards:** two stacked. A 60 x 34 mm 2-oz power board carries the XT60PW + XT30PW males at the battery end and the females at the drone end (rib between each pair). A 50 x 30 mm logic board with the screen, ring LEDs and Power switch sits 7 mm above it on a 2 x 6 board-to-board header. USB-C is on the back flank.

**UX:** press anywhere on the glass to check and power on, and again to turn off. The ring and 0.96" colour IPS words light through dead-front glass. Bind and Limit are two keys on the front flank, named above them; Limit steps are shown on screen, and 20 A asks for a second press. First use: push it onto the battery, push the drone's lead in, press the glass. Answers pain point 7 (no leads to rip off) and gives the shortest current path (~10 mΩ).

**Changes to the spec:** no drone leads. A 10 cm XT60 extension in the box covers frame-mounted connectors.

**Cost delta:** about +$3.00 (machined body, glass, flexure, second board), minus $0.45 for the leads.

**Risks:** leverage on the drone's pigtail; a glass key needs a well-tuned flexure; the spare inputs are live but shrouded.

**Scores (1-5):** Premium 5 · Noob-proof 5 · Bench practicality 3 · Build quality 5 · Uniqueness 4
