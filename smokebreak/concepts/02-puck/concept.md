# 02 Puck

**Pitch:** a heavy aluminium puck with a glass top. The Beacon Ring glows under the glass around a round screen. Press the glass to power on. The ring's dot is a real metal button, and it is Bind.

**Form & size:** Ø80 × 22 mm, about 150 g.
- Black bead-blasted, anodised 6061 aluminium shell, CNC-machined, with flats at 9 o'clock (battery) and 3 o'clock (drone).
- A Ø66 × 2 mm toughened glass top, printed on the back.
- A rubber foot ring underneath.
- Batch 1: CNC aluminium (or an MJF nylon shell for samples). Later: die-cast or forged and machined.

**Boards:**
- **One round 4-layer board, Ø74, 2 oz outer layers.** The FETs, shunt and pulse resistors are underneath, pressed onto a boss in the base through a gap pad. The aluminium base is the heatsink.
- The display, on its FPC, sits under the glass. The light guide sits over 12 RGB LEDs.
- XT60PW + XT30PW male are on the board edge at the left flat, with a rib between them. The drone leads leave through glands in the right flat. USB-C is on the back rim.

**UX:**
- **Power:** press anywhere on the glass. It floats on one dome switch and travels 0.4 mm, like a trackpad.
- **Bind:** press the Ring's dot (stainless, glows blue).
- **Limit:** a stainless pill on the front rim. Each click lights the next mark on the printed scale (AUTO 1 2 5 10 20), and 20 A needs a second click.
- **Screen:** a 1.28" round IPS (240 × 240) gives large centred words, more readable than the 0.96" OLED.
- **First use:** 1 Battery in, 2 Drone in, 3 Press the glass.
- **Why it's noob-proof:** the biggest thing is the thing you press.
- **Pains answered (SPEC §2):** best on 6 (confusing LEDs) and 7 (closed, solid, nothing to rip off).

**Cost delta:** about +$7 (machined shell +$3.5, glass +$0.8, round IPS +$1.5, light guide and 12 LEDs +$0.6, bigger board +$0.6).

**Risks:**
- Glass-top switch feel needs tuning.
- Batch 1 machining cost.
- The leads can drag a light puck (it needs the weight).
- The glass must be toughened.

**Scores:** Premium 5 · Noob-proof 5 · Bench practicality 4 · Build quality 5 · Uniqueness 4
