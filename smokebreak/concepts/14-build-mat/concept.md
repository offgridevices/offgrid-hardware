# 14 Build Mat

**Pitch:** the smoke stopper is the corner of a 400 × 260 mm silicone build mat. The mat has a bay for the battery, a circle for the drone and a groove for the leads, so the whole bench is laid out around the check.

**Form & size:** the mat is 400 × 260 × 3 mm platinum silicone (heat-proof to 260 °C, ESD-dissipative) with a Bone print: steps, drone circle, battery bay, lockup. A raised rim keeps the battery in its bay. The pod is 96 × 64 × 14 mm, ≈ 140 g, machined 6061 with glass over the screen. It sits in a moulded pocket and lifts out to use alone. The mat rolls up for travel.

**Boards:** one, 92 × 58 mm, 2 oz. The top carries a 1.9" IPS, the ring LEDs and 8 keys. The bottom carries the power path, with a copper slug into the aluminium floor. XT60PW + XT30PW (male) face the bay on the back edge. 25 cm drone leads leave the left side into the groove. USB-C is on the right side.

**UX:** **Power** is a domed key inside the Beacon Ring. **Limit** is six direct-select keys (AUTO 1 2 5 10 20), and the lit one is the setting, so there is no cycling. The 20 key has an Ember rim and needs a 1 s hold plus "Props off?" on screen. **Bind** has its own key. First use: battery in the bay, drone on the circle, press Power. It is noob-proof because everything has a printed place and every option is visible. It answers pain points 4, 7 and 6 best.

**Cost delta:** pod ≈ +$5, mat ≈ +$5. Sell it as a kit (≈ $49), with the pod also sold alone.

**Risks:** the silicone insulates the pod. The aluminium mass takes the 20 A × 30 s heat (≈ 170 J, a rise of about 4 °C). Shipping volume. Needs bench space.
