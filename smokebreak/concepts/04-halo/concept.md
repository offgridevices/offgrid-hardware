# 04 Halo

**Pitch:** the product is the OffGrid mark: a C-shaped aluminium ring with the Beacon node standing in its gap. The node is Power and the ring is the status light. Touch the printed number to set Limit.

**Form & size:** Ø96 mm, band 24 mm, 18 mm tall. The Ø20 node stands 22 mm tall on a recessed bridge, so it reads as floating. About 160 g.
- Hollow, CNC-machined black anodised aluminium.
- A frosted light channel along the inner edge. Glass over the screen.
- Batch 1: CNC, or painted MJF. Later: die-cast and machined.

**Boards:**
- **One C-shaped 4-layer board** (OD 92, ID 52, 2 oz outer layers). The power path sits in the lower arc on a gap pad over the base, and the ring is the heatsink.
- XT60PW + XT30PW male are on the outer wall at 9 o'clock, stacked with a rib. The leads leave at 3 o'clock and USB-C is at 5 o'clock.
- A Ø16 node board (Power and its LEDs) is joined by a flex through the bridge.
- Capacitive pads sit under the Limit numbers.

**UX:**
- **Power:** press the dot.
- **Limit:** touch AUTO, 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 on the left arc, and your choice lights up. 20 asks for a second touch: props off.
- **Bind:** a stainless button on the right arc.
- **Screen:** a 1.14" IPS at 6 o'clock, facing you. The whole ring glows the status.
- **First use:** 1 Battery in, 2 Drone in, 3 Press the dot.
- **Why it's noob-proof:** the current visibly flows round the ring from battery to drone.
- **Pains answered (SPEC §2):** 6 and 4.

**Cost delta:** about +$10 (ring and node +$5, board panel waste +$1.2, touch and LEDs +$0.6, IPS and glass +$1.5, flex +$0.4).

**Risks:**
- Touch pads need glass or PMMA inlays.
- Accidental touches.
- The largest footprint.
- A thin bridge would look fragile.

**Scores:** Premium 5 · Noob-proof 4 · Bench practicality 3 · Build quality 4 · Uniqueness 5
